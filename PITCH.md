PulseMesh is a civic ops desk that ingests frantic neighborhood reports and instantly outputs an ICS-ready plan—timeline, resource gaps, and message cues—so responders can act in minutes, not hours. Unlike dashboards that only monitor sensors, we fuse human observations with AI reasoning, validate the response against a JSON schema, and render decision-grade widgets that command centers can trust. The experience is deployable on Vercel, embeddable in judge portals, and demonstrates how AI copilots can orchestrate hyperlocal resilience for any city.
